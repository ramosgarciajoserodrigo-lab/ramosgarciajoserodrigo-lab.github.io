About me

The person behind the projects

I'm Rodrigo, from Puebla. I grew up there, spent my secondary school years in Wolfsburg, Germany, and did my first two years of the degree at Tec de Monterrey in Puebla. This semester I moved to Monterrey to continue it.

I chose Industrial & Systems Engineering partly because of my dad, but mostly because it fits me: since I was a kid I've liked math, processes, analyzing how things work and thinking outside the box.

What I enjoy most is meeting people who think and analyze in different ways, and working with data to solve real problems. I also like explaining: I've tutored math at Kumon and taught AI workshops to kids and teenagers.
